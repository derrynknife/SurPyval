Non-Parametric

Estimators that make no assumption about the shape of the distribution: the survival curve is read straight from the data. Each estimator is exported as a ready-made instance – KaplanMeier, NelsonAalen, FlemingHarrington and Turnbull – whose fit(x, c, n, t) returns a NonParametric model. That model carries the survival, hazard and quantile functions, confidence bounds and bands, the restricted mean and plotting.

The theory is in Non-Parametric Estimation and worked examples are in Non-Parametric SurPyval Modelling. The log-rank test and the two-group restricted-mean difference, which compare non-parametric estimates between groups, are documented in Comparison Tests and Validation Metrics.

Non-Parametric Class

The fitted model every estimator returns.

  • Non-Parametric Model

Non-Parametric Estimators

Kaplan-Meier, Nelson-Aalen and Fleming-Harrington handle observed, right-censored and left-truncated data, and raise a ValueError on left- or interval-censored or right-truncated data; Turnbull handles every combination of censoring and truncation.

  • Kaplan-Meier
  • Nelson-Aalen
  • Fleming-Harrington
  • Turnbull

Relative Survival

Net survival beyond a population’s own hazards, read from a rate table: Pohar Perme’s estimator and Ederer II, and the excess hazard regression beside them. See Relative Survival for the guide.

  • Relative Survival

Plotting

Several estimates on one figure, with the number at risk under it.

  • Plotting Non-Parametric Estimates

Other Non-Parametric Functions

Zero-failure (success-run) testing, the plotting positions used by probability plots and by the MPP parametric fitting method, exposure (person-years) tables of event rates by factor and time band, and the pooling of event rates (or probabilities per trial) across sources.
