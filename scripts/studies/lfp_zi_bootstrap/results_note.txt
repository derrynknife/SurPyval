Bootstrap coverage of limited-failure (lfp) and zero-inflated (zi) fits:
the package's parametric bootstrap against a case bootstrap.

Run (5 November 2026): study.py 400, then summary.py. Replicate r of each
design is the r-th data set drawn from the calibration study's seed
(311-314), n = 100 units, so replicates 0-199 are the calibration data
exactly; 400 replicates per design (1,600 fits, 0 refits failed in either
bootstrap), n_boot = 200, 90% and 95% two-sided intervals.
The work plan was 1,000 replicates at n_boot = 500; at that size one
replicate of the four designs costs about 1,250 core-seconds (about 35
hours on ten cores), so the study was cut to 400 replicates at the
calibration's n_boot = 200 (about 55 core-seconds a replicate, run in
well under the three-hour budget). Monte Carlo error of one coverage cell
at 400 replicates: sqrt(p (1 - p) / 400) = 0.015 at 0.90, 0.011 at 0.95
(0.021 / 0.015 at the calibration's 200). Per-cell numbers with their
errors and median widths are in summary.json (all 400) and
summary_first200.json (the calibration's 200 data sets; param200 there
reproduces the DECISIONS.md figures: lfp 0.88-0.925, lfp_edge 0.855-0.86,
zi 0.855-0.91, lognormal_lfp 0.875-0.91). Not studied: the sf and qf
bounds the calibration also checks (parameters only, which is where the
shortfall is clearest).

Methods
  param200    the package's parametric bootstrap, BCa with the jackknife
              acceleration (the calibration's bounds)
  case200     units resampled with replacement, same refit, lfp_p / f0 put
              on their edge as the package does, same BCa
  casepct200  the plain percentile interval of the case refits

Findings
1. The shortfall is small but looks real for the distribution parameters.
   All 8 of them (alpha, beta, mu, sigma over the four designs) are below
   0.90 at 90% (0.863-0.897, mean 0.878) and below 0.95 at 95%
   (0.917-0.938, mean 0.930); a single cell (error 0.015) could be noise,
   but eight of eight on the same side at both levels is not likely to be.
   It is a point or two to three points, the usual small-sample bootstrap
   shortfall at 100 units with about 60 failures (about 40 in the lfp
   design). The extra parameters (lfp_p off the edge, f0) are at or above
   nominal at 90% (0.91-0.92) and at 95% (0.94-0.97).
2. Case resampling does not fix it, and nowhere near the edge does it
   help: its distribution-parameter coverage is the same or a little
   lower (distribution parameters: mean 0.863 against 0.878 at 90%,
   0.916 against 0.930 at 95%),
   and the plain percentile interval is lower still. The edge design's
   alpha and beta are 0.85 with case resampling against 0.87 with the
   model bootstrap. lfp_p on the edge is about 0.99-1.00 either way
   (target 0.95 at 90%): the interval reaches the edge in nearly every
   replicate, conservative rather than short.
3. The 200-replicate calibration cells are consistent with this: the
   same param200 cells at 400 replicates are within noise of them.

Recommendation (a proposal; nothing here changes behaviour)
- Keep the parametric bootstrap with BCa as it is. Case resampling is no
  better for these models and costs the same.
- Do not raise n_boot to fix this: the BCa interval at 200 refits is not
  limited by draw noise (500 draws were not run; the shortfall is the
  same across designs and levels, which points to the estimator's finite
  sample behaviour, not to Monte Carlo error in the interval ends). A
  500-draw run of the same data would confirm that if wanted.
- If a better small-sample interval is wanted, the candidates are a
  bootstrap-t / studentised interval, or profile-likelihood bounds that
  respect the boundary (for lfp_p and f0 the likelihood-ratio bounds
  already do, and the calibration for them passes).
- Worth a warning, as a proposal: a limited-failure or zero-inflated
  fit with few failures (say under about 30) could say its bootstrap
  intervals run a point or two short; this study did not vary the number
  of failures, so the threshold is a guess to be set by a further study.

Calibration group (proposal only, nothing changed). The bootstrap test is
already a group of its own in .github/workflows/actions.yml
(extra-parameters-bootstrap, 47 minutes locally against a 60-minute
limit). Its four parametrised cases cost, in this study's timings, about
(per replicate, relative) lfp_edge 560, lognormal_lfp 290, lfp 280, zi 130.
Two groups, [lfp_edge, zi] and [lfp, lognormal_lfp], would be about 55%
and 45% of the time (roughly 26 and 21 minutes), using pytest ids
test_bootstrap_coverage[lfp_edge] etc. in the matrix's args. The
studies share no state and every seed is per design, so the results do
not change.
