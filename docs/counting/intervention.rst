Intervention Test
=================

Did an intervention -- a design change, a new procedure, a corrective
action -- change the rate of failures of one repairable system or a
fleet, and is the evidence enough to say? Worked examples are in
:doc:`../Intervention Analysis with SurPyval`; the power and exposure
functions behind the evidence are in :doc:`../surpyval.test_planning`.
